# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designers or Researchers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and Researchers help teams understand user needs, validate experience decisions, and ensure the product is usable, accessible, and aligned with customer expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user interviews, usability testing, and journey mapping
- Translate user insights into UX requirements and design decisions
- Collaborate with Product Managers to refine problem statements and success criteria
- Partner with Developers and QA to validate usability, accessibility, and edge cases
- Support design reviews and maintain design consistency across workflows

### Goals
- Improve customer experience and adoption
- Reduce friction in critical user journeys
- Ensure product decisions are grounded in evidence and usability

### Typical Communication
- Product discovery sessions and user research reviews
- UX critiques and design sign-offs
- Design handoff documentation and acceptance discussions with engineering and QA

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Product Managers to define user outcomes and priorities
- Partners with Developers to ensure implementation matches intended UX
- Coordinates with QA to validate usability, accessibility, and regression risk
- Escalates design trade-offs or user risk to the Product Manager and Project Manager when they affect scope, timeline, or stakeholder expectations

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical direction, design integrity, and delivery quality so that solutions are maintainable, scalable, and aligned with team constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture, standards, and implementation patterns
- Review major design decisions and technical trade-offs
- Support Developers with technical guidance, code review, and risk mitigation
- Identify dependencies, integration constraints, and long-term platform concerns
- Help prioritize technical debt and quality improvements alongside delivery goals

### Goals
- Deliver robust, maintainable, and scalable solutions
- Reduce avoidable rework and architecture drift
- Improve confidence in delivery quality and operational readiness

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical design discussions
- Engineering planning and dependency alignment
- Escalation of technical risks, blockers, and mitigation plans

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supports Product Managers and Project Managers by clarifying feasibility, dependencies, and risk
- Advises Developers on design choices and implementation quality
- Aligns with QA on testability, reliability, and risk-based validation
- Escalates cross-team or delivery risks to the Project Manager when timeline, quality, or architectural decisions require coordination

---

## Product Owners (when distinct from Product Managers)

### Role Summary
A Product Owner may act as the day-to-day decision-maker for backlog prioritization, delivery readiness, and value realization when the team uses a separate ownership model from the Product Manager.

### Responsibilities
- Prioritize backlog items based on team capacity and product value
- Clarify requirements and acceptance criteria for delivery teams
- Coordinate release readiness with cross-functional stakeholders
- Accept or reject work as it meets agreed definitions of done
- Maintain alignment between roadmap intent and delivery execution

### Goals
- Increase delivery clarity and throughput
- Ensure the team is working on the highest-value work
- Improve quality of refinement and acceptance decisions

### Typical Communication
- Backlog refinement and sprint planning
- Acceptance reviews and prioritization discussions
- Release readiness check-ins with PM, engineering, and QA

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to maintain strategic alignment and customer value
- Partners with Developers and QA on backlog clarification and acceptance
- Coordinates with Project Managers on dependencies, stakeholder communication, and risk visibility
- Escalates trade-offs that affect scope or priority to the Product Manager when strategic direction is involved

---

## Delivery / Release Managers

### Role Summary
Delivery or Release Managers coordinate readiness, deployment, and operational execution so that work can move from implementation to production with clear ownership and reduced release risk.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release plans, cutover timing, and communication
- Track deployment readiness across engineering, QA, and operations
- Ensure rollback, verification, and support plans are documented
- Monitor release dependencies and cross-team sequencing
- Help manage risk and communication during launch windows

### Goals
- Minimize release disruption and incident risk
- Improve predictability of deployments and operational readiness
- Align schedules and milestones across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and launch planning
- Deployment notes and stakeholder updates
- Incident or rollback coordination with project and support teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to manage calendar, dependencies, and stakeholder expectations
- Coordinates with Developers and QA to confirm feature readiness and validation status
- Supports Product Managers and Stakeholders with launch communications and release impact awareness
- Escalates release blocking issues to the Project Manager and relevant leadership if deployment risk or customer impact rises

---

## Security / Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads ensure that risks, compliance needs, and data protection requirements are addressed early and consistently throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review project requirements for security and privacy risk
- Define or validate secure design patterns and compliance requirements
- Assess risks and remediation plans for data handling, access, and third-party dependencies
- Support incident response planning and security review gates
- Collaborate with engineering and product teams on risk-aware decision making

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk
- Build trust with customers, stakeholders, and regulators
- Embed security and privacy into delivery practices early

### Typical Communication
- Security design reviews and risk assessments
- Compliance and privacy checkpoints
- Incident readiness and response coordination

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Advises Product Managers on risk trade-offs and requirement gaps
- Partners with Developers and Technical Leads on secure architecture and implementation
- Coordinates with QA on validation of security and privacy controls
- Escalates material risk or policy gaps to Project Managers and Sponsor-level stakeholders when they affect delivery or exposure

---

## Customer Support / Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Operations representatives ensure that delivery decisions account for real-world supportability, service quality, and customer impact after launch.

### Responsibilities
- Provide input on supportability, escalation paths, and operational readiness
- Share recurring customer issues or service challenges with the project team
- Help document support processes, runbooks, and handoff requirements
- Review release impact for support load and customer communication needs
- Contribute to post-release follow-up and incident learning

### Goals
- Improve operational continuity and service quality
- Reduce support burden and customer confusion
- Ensure releases are ready for real-world use

### Typical Communication
- Supportability reviews and readiness check-ins
- User issue summaries and escalation coordination
- Post-release feedback and incident reporting

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers and Project Managers to reflect customer impact and support constraints
- Coordinates with Developers and QA on bug triage, validation, and service readiness
- Aligns with Release Managers on communication timing and rollout support
- Escalates customer-impacting issues to the Project Manager and broader stakeholders when mitigation or prioritization is needed

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- This expanded set helps teams clarify accountability, ownership, and decision-making across planning, execution, release, and operations.
